# BIM Knowledge Engineer Skill

## Overview
The **BIM Knowledge Engineer** maintains the domain ontology, regulatory rules, structural standards, and interactive learning checkpoints across BIMPlan Academy.

## Key Responsibilities & Capabilities
- **ÇŞB & Market Poz Mapping:** Maintains unit price databases (e.g. DEMO-15.120.1001, C35/45 Ready Mix Concrete, B420C Structural Steel).
- **Eurocode 2 & Structural Knowledge:** Encapsulates EN 1992-1-1 rebar lap lengths, anchorage coefficients ($\alpha$), and bond quality factors ($\eta_1$).
- **Interactive Checkpoints & Quiz Engine:** Generates real-time precision questions (`InlineCheckpoint.tsx`) and 5-subject certification exams (`ExamInterface.tsx`).
- **BIM Glossary & Standards Taxonomy:** Governs terminology for LOD, LOIN, BCF, IFC2x3, IFC4, and Allplan Bimplus cloud workflows.

## Best Practices
- Keep all regulatory citations grounded in official legislation (e.g. 6306 Kentsel Dönüşüm Kanunu, Eurocode 2).
- Ensure quiz questions provide technical explanations pointing out exact structural engineering clauses.
