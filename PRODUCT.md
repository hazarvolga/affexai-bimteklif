# BIMPlan Academy Product Context

## Register

product

## Users

The first commercial users are Turkish construction companies with small and mid-sized estimating or technical-office teams. The buyer is usually the owner, technical manager, or estimating lead; the daily user is the estimator or quantity-surveying specialist.

Their job is to prepare offers quickly, keep quantities and unit prices consistent, protect margin, and explain or correct errors before an offer is sent.

## Product Purpose

BIMPlan Academy is the umbrella for a construction-work intelligence ecosystem.

The first paid product is TeklifPro: a reliable workspace for clients, quotations, quantity items, pricing, PDF output, and profitability visibility.

SmartCheck is TeklifPro's quality layer. It makes quotation and quantity issues visible, records evidence, guides correction, and verifies the result again.

Academy is the learning and enablement layer. It first exists to reduce support cost, improve correct product usage, and connect a finding to a short practical lesson. It is not the first standalone paid LMS.

BIMPLAN is a future technical standards and rule-source layer. ALLPLAN Academy is a future product academy inside Academy, not a separate learning platform.

## Commercial Wedge

Sell TeklifPro first. Use SmartCheck as the premium differentiator and Academy as trust, onboarding, and retention support.

The first commercial promise is:

> Prepare construction offers faster, protect your margin, and see errors before they become expensive.

The first proof mechanism is not a generic AI claim. It is measured improvement in real pilot workflows: time to first quote, quote completion rate, calculation errors, PDF reliability, support effort, and continued paid usage.

## Brand Personality

- Institutional confidence
- Practical engineering discipline
- Clear and accountable

The product should feel like a dependable technical work system, not an experimental AI toy.

## Anti-references

- No unsupported "Turkey's first" claims.
- No fake customer logos or placeholder references.
- No promise that AI, BIM, or legislation is automatically authoritative.
- No public pricing until the pilot economics and support burden are validated.
- No launch message that presents TeklifPro, SmartCheck, Academy, and BIMPLAN as four finished products.
- No engineering calculation or standards-compliance claim without versioned source evidence and qualified human review.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence before hype:** Every important claim needs a visible proof mechanism.
2. **Commercial focus before platform breadth:** One paid workflow must work before ecosystem expansion.
3. **Human approval for technical decisions:** SmartCheck can surface and organize issues; it must not silently declare engineering compliance.
4. **Progressive disclosure:** TeklifPro is the entry point; SmartCheck and Academy appear when they help the user's current work.
5. **Trust is a feature:** Show provenance, version, calculation boundaries, and limitations directly in the interface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the public and product surfaces. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, non-color-only status communication, reduced-motion behavior, and Turkish-language clarity.
